Common Questions

Do I really have to start with the free audit?

Yes — and it's for your benefit as much as ours. We won't scope or price work without understanding your actual operations first. The audit prevents misalignment on both sides.

What if my needs don't fit neatly into a tier?

That's what the findings call is for. The tiers are starting points — scope is always refined based on what the audit reveals. We don't force you into a box.

How long does a typical engagement last?

Foundations engagements are typically 4–8 weeks. Infrastructure retainers run 3–6 months. Transformation engagements are scoped per project and can range from 2–12 months.

We're a solopreneur — is this really for us?

Yes. The earlier you build operational infrastructure, the cheaper it is. Building systems at 1 person is far easier than rebuilding broken ones at 20. The Foundations tier exists exactly for this.

Do you work with brands outside of manufacturing?

Absolutely. While our background is in regulated manufacturing, operational infrastructure applies to any industry. If you have processes, we can improve them.

What happens after an engagement ends?

You're handed documented systems, trained staff, and a clear operational roadmap. Past clients can re-engage for specific projects or advisory as their needs evolve.

Do you build AI tools, or just advise on AI strategy?

We build. Every AI engagement starts the same way our ops work does — we map the actual process first, then build the automation it needs. No generic AI strategy decks.

Is AI a separate engagement, or part of the existing tiers?

It's part of the same audit and the same tiers. If AI is the right fix for something we find, it's scoped and built alongside everything else — not sold as a separate add-on.
