Common Questions

What families ask us most.

Is this the same as a home health aide or a nurse?

No. Home health aides and nurses provide clinical and physical care. We provide the operational and coordination layer that sits around that care — the planning, the logistics, the home setup, the family alignment, and the ongoing navigation that clinical staff don't have time for and families don't know how to do.

Do you give medical advice?

No. We translate medical instructions into practical daily systems — we don't provide medical guidance. We work alongside your medical team, not in place of them.

What if we're not sure which service is right for us?

That's exactly what the free assessment is for. Tell us what's happening. We'll tell you honestly what level of support makes sense — and if something simpler would serve you better, we'll say so.

Can you help if the situation is urgent?

Yes. The Stabilization Visit is specifically designed for rapid-response situations. Reach out directly at support@giltcp.com and we will respond the same day.

Do you travel for in-home visits?

Yes. Travel throughout the continental United States is included in the Premium Concierge offering and available as an add-on for other engagements. We are not limited by geography.

What happens after the engagement ends?

You keep everything we build — the care plan, the vendor contacts, the documented routines. Many families transition from a structured engagement into Monthly Navigation when the acute phase has passed. There's no pressure to continue, and the door is always open.
