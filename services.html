Common Questions

What families ask most.

Do I have to pay anything before the first call?

No. The 30-minute investigation call is completely free. Nothing is charged until after that conversation, and only if you choose to move forward. There is no pressure and no obligation.

What is the difference between a virtual and in-person evaluation?

The virtual Stabilization Evaluation covers everything the in-person visit does, conducted over secure video with a camera walkthrough of the home. The in-person visit allows hands-on assessment and on-site fixes. Most families start with the virtual option and upgrade if needed.

Is this the same as a home health aide or a nurse?

No. We provide the operational and coordination layer that sits around clinical care. The planning, the logistics, the home setup, the family alignment, and the ongoing navigation that medical staff do not have time for and families do not know how to do.

Do you give medical advice?

No. We translate medical instructions into practical daily systems. We work alongside your medical team, never in place of them.

Can you help if the situation is urgent?

Yes. Email support@giltcp.com directly and we will respond the same day. The free investigation call can be scheduled within 24 to 48 hours in urgent situations.

What happens after an engagement ends?

You keep everything we build together. The care plan, the vendor contacts, the documented routines. Many families transition into Monthly Navigation when the acute phase has passed. There is no pressure to continue and no penalty for stopping.
